Discovery meeting — Sanjay with Alex, owner of a plumbing company in Lewisville.

SANJAY: Walk me through how a new customer usually reaches you.
ALEX: They call. That's it really. Website has a form but honestly I check
that email maybe once a week. Most days I'm under a house until 6.
SANJAY: What happens to calls while you're on a job?
ALEX: Voicemail. My wife used to pick up but she works now. I'd say I miss
half my calls after 4pm and all of them on weekends.
SANJAY: What's an average job worth?
ALEX: Small stuff 250, water heaters 1500-2000.
SANJAY: What eats your evenings?
ALEX: Invoices. I write them from memory off my job notes at like 9pm in
QuickBooks. Half the time I forget a part I used.
SANJAY: If we fixed one thing first?
ALEX: The calls. I know I'm losing water heater jobs to the big guys with
answering services. But don't have it cancel anything without asking me.
SANJAY: Hours are what, 8 to 5 weekdays?
ALEX: Yeah, 8 to 5 Monday through Friday, Lewisville and Flower Mound mostly.
